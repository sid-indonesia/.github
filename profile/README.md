The **Summit Institute for Development (SID)** (Yayasan Institut Pengembangan Suara Mitra) is a non-profit research and public health implementation organization in Indonesia. We leverage epidemiological research, low-resource digital health interventions, and cloud-native open-source technology to improve maternal, neonatal, and child health outcomes.

---

## 🔬 Core Focus Areas

- **Maternal & Child Health (MNCH):** Large-scale field implementation and community-based health interventions across Indonesia.
- **Digital Health Systems:** Deploying, customizing, and scaling open-source digital solutions (OpenSRP, FHIR, KoBoToolbox) for frontline healthcare workers and midwives.
- **Data Science & ML Integration:** Integrating computer vision and machine learning for automated analysis of health indicators, maternal handbooks, and clinical records.
- **Global Research:** Partnering with international research institutions, multilateral agencies, and government health authorities to deliver evidence-based interventions.
